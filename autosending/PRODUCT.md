# AutoSending

## Product Purpose
A Telegram automation SaaS dashboard for digital marketers running outreach campaigns at scale. Users connect multiple Telegram accounts (via Telethon), define target channels and message templates, then launch campaigns that auto-send messages with randomized delays. The product handles account rotation, status monitoring, and activity logging.

## Users
Russian-speaking digital marketers and SMM specialists managing outreach campaigns. Technically literate but not developers. They work late (11pm on a desktop monitor is normal). They care about reliability, seeing campaign status at a glance, and not getting banned. Time-sensitive: a stuck campaign costs real money.

## Tone
Direct. No corporate warmth, no cheerful copy. Professional and functional. Russian language throughout the UI.

## Strategic Principles
- Speed over decoration: users need to see campaign status immediately on load
- Trust through clarity: data that's hard to read is worse than no data
- Keyboard-friendly flows for power users
- No dark patterns, no artificial loading states

## Anti-references
- No Telegram's own blue everywhere (product must feel independent)
- No generic SaaS dashboards that look like every other tool
- No gradients on text
- No glassmorphism panels
- No hero-metric cards (big number + gradient accent)
- Not another "dark blue with neon accents" tool
